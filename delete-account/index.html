Delete your SEVI account

SEVI is the app for buying and selling homes in Greece, developed by the SEVI team. This page explains how to delete your SEVI account and your data.

Delete your account in the app

In SEVI, open Profile, tap Delete account, read what we delete and what we keep, then confirm. Your account is deleted at once and you’re logged out. If you haven’t finished signing up, the same Delete account is at the foot of the sign-up step.

On an iPhone, if you sign in with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm first, and SEVI then stops using your Apple ID.

Or ask us by email

If you can’t use the app, ask us by email:

  1. Write to privacy@sevi.homes from the email address you use to sign in to SEVI.
  2. Ask us to delete your account. You don’t need to give a reason.
  3. If you sign in with your phone number, or can’t write from that address (for example, because you use Apple’s Hide My Email), tell us how you sign in. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours.
  4. We answer within one month, and delete your data as described below.

What we delete

Your account and the personal data linked to it, without undue delay. This includes:

  • your profile: your first and last name, your email address or phone number, how you sign in, your photo, your language and your acceptance of our terms;
  • your search, the homes you liked or passed, your interests and your buyer card;
  • your listings, with their photos, details and documents, and your verifications.

If you sign in with Apple, Google, Facebook or Microsoft, deleting your SEVI account doesn’t delete that account. You can also remove SEVI from the apps that can use it, in its settings.

What we keep, and for how long

  • What the law requires us to keep, for as long as it requires it.
  • Backups, where kept, roll over within 7 days, so any copy of your data in them is gone within 7 days of its deletion.
  • App events, no longer linked to your account, for up to 13 months from when they happened. After that, only totals that don’t identify anyone.
  • Connections. If you were connected with someone, the record of that Connection stays on their side, no longer linked to your account.
  • Reports. Reports you made, or that were made about you, with what was reported at the time, stay for as long as needed for moderation and safety.
  • Our team’s log of the actions it took on your account and your listings, for as long as needed for moderation and safety.
  • Security logs, which include IP addresses, for as long as needed for security.
  • Introduction emails. If SEVI’s team introduced you to someone by email during the pilot, that email stays in your inbox and theirs, which we can’t reach.

Deleting only some of your data

To delete some of your data and keep your account (one of your listings, for example), write to privacy@sevi.homes and tell us what to delete.
