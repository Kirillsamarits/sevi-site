1. Who we are

SEVI connects people who want to buy a home in Greece with people who want to sell one. It is run by Sevi LLC, a company in formation that is being registered in Athens, Greece, called “SEVI”, “we” or “us” here. Our postal address: Arkadias 38, 166 75 Glyfada, Athens, Greece. We will add the company's registration and VAT numbers here once it is registered.

You can reach us through Help & feedback in your Profile, or by email to legal@sevi.homes. If the company that runs SEVI changes, we will tell you in the app first.

2. Agreeing to these terms

  • When you tick the boxes on Before you start and tap I agree, you accept these Terms of Use and the Disclaimers, and you confirm that you have read the Privacy Policy. If you don't agree, please don't use SEVI.
  • You must be 18 or older and able to make a binding agreement.
  • We record which version of these texts you accepted, and when.

3. What SEVI is, and what it is not

  • Buyers browse homes and tell sellers they are interested. Sellers see who is interested and choose whom to connect with. When a seller says yes, you are connected. Where messaging is switched on, you can then message each other in SEVI. While it is not, and for people who connected while it was not, SEVI's team introduces connected people to each other by email.
  • SEVI is not an estate agent, and we are not a party to any sale, lease or other agreement between users. We don't negotiate, hold deposits or money, draft contracts or give legal, tax, technical or financial advice.
  • Any agreement you make with another user is between you and them.

4. Your account

  • One account per person, in your own real name, with a real photo of you.
  • Keep your sign-in details safe. You are responsible for what is done with your account.
  • Keep your details accurate. We may ask you to confirm your phone number, your identity or, if you sell, that you own the property or may sell it.
  • You can ask us to delete your account at any time through Help & feedback, or by email to privacy@sevi.homes from your account's email address, or delete it yourself in the app: Profile, then Delete account.

5. Rules for everyone

You agree not to:

  • give false information, pretend to be someone else, or list a property you have no right to sell;
  • harass, threaten, deceive or discriminate against anyone;
  • send spam or advertising, or use SEVI for anything other than buying or selling a home;
  • put contact details, addresses or links in photos, descriptions or messages before you are connected;
  • copy, scrape or collect listings or other users' data, or try to break, overload or get around how SEVI works;
  • upload anything unlawful, or anything that infringes someone else's rights.

Messages

  • Messages are between you and the person you are connected with. Where messaging is switched on, you can write to each other once you are connected. Be honest and polite, and keep to the rules above.
  • Deleting a message. You can delete a message you sent, for both of you, within 5 minutes of sending it. We keep a private copy for 90 days, so that our team can deal with reports.
  • Sharing your contact details. Your email address, phone number or a video call link reach the other person only if you choose to share them. That is your own decision, and a shared detail can't be taken back. Nobody has to share theirs.
  • Video calls happen on the service whose link you share (such as Google Meet or Zoom), under that service's own terms. SEVI is not part of the call.
  • Report and block. You can report a message, and you can block a person: you then no longer see each other on SEVI, and the conversation closes for both of you. The other person is not told.
  • What our team sees. Our team does not read your conversations. They see a message only when it is reported, or when the law requires it.

6. Sellers and listings

  • List only a property you own, or one you are authorised in writing to sell. Describe it truthfully: the total asking price in euros, its size, rooms, condition, the energy certificate (ΠΕΑ) where the law requires one, and any Golden Visa basis you claim. Your photos, edited or not, must show it as it is.
  • We check every listing before it goes live, as described under “How we check listings” below. We may ask for changes or documents, and refuse or remove a listing, in particular if it may be inaccurate, a duplicate, unlawful or not yours to sell. We may ask for proof that the home is yours (a land registry extract or E9) before a buyer can connect with you. Our checks reduce risk; they are not a guarantee.
  • The exact address and location stay private until you connect with a buyer. Before that, buyers see an approximate area.
  • When you connect with a buyer, they see your name and what has been verified about you.

How we check listings

  • Automated checks look at every listing: its photos and public details, with Google's Gemini AI, and its price per m², compared with reference prices for its area (such as official objective values or published market price indices or, until we have verified those, our own estimates) and with a national range.
  • A price far from similar homes can be submitted only after you change it or add a short note that explains why; a person then checks it. About two days after a listing goes live we may tell you, as advice, that its price looks well above or below similar homes; the listing stays live.
  • Photo fingerprints: we compare a fingerprint of your photos with other sellers' listings to find copied listings.
  • Going live: a listing that passes all the checks can go live without a person looking at it first. Our team may check it later and can take it back into review, or back for changes.
  • A person decides: if a check finds a problem, a person reviews the listing. A person makes every refusal or request for changes and tells you which rule, why and how to fix it; you can ask us to look again at support@sevi.homes.

7. Your photos and listing content

  • You keep ownership of what you upload.
  • You confirm that you took the photos, or that you hold a written licence from the photographer that allows online use, sublicensing to our service providers and changes such as resizing, cropping, enhancement and editing with AI tools. Photos must not show people (including in mirrors and reflections), house numbers, number plates or documents.
  • You give SEVI a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence, which we may pass on only to the providers who run SEVI for us, to host, store, copy, resize, crop and compress your listing content, to make versions of it (thumbnails, edited versions, previews for sharing) and to show it in the app and on SEVI's property web pages.
  • The licence lasts while the listing can be seen under our access rules (including by buyers who liked it, for 30 days after it stops being live), and then for the retention periods in the Privacy Policy.
  • Edited photos. With Tidy up you can remove movable things from a photo, such as dishes, bins, cables, clothes or bags, and we correct the light, contrast and colour.
  • An edit may only remove things you would put away before a viewing. It must never change the property itself: no hiding damage, damp or cracks; no removing or adding walls, doors, windows, fixtures and fittings, or furniture and appliances included in the sale; no removing power lines, roads or neighbouring buildings; no changing views; no adding pools, gardens or renovations; no making rooms look larger; and no adding people.
  • You are responsible for the edits you keep, and for making sure they don't give buyers a misleading picture of the home. We keep the original of every edited photo. It carries an “Edited” label, and buyers can always see the original. If you use an edited photo elsewhere, say that it is edited.
  • We review edited photos with the listing. We may refuse an edit, show the original instead, or ask for a different photo if an edit may mislead buyers or breaks these rules.
  • Edited photos are versions of your photos. We pass to you any rights we have in them and keep the licence above. Tidy up is free during the pilot.
  • Our providers may not use your content to train their models. We will not use your content in our marketing, such as our social media, unless you separately agree, and you can withdraw that agreement at any time.
  • The photographer's moral rights stay with the photographer.
  • Suggested descriptions. A suggested description is a draft written from your details. You are responsible for what your listing says: check it and change anything that isn't right before you submit.

8. Buyers

  • When you like a home, the seller can see your buyer card: your first name, your budget band, your areas of interest and the details you choose to add. Your photo, last name, languages and country are shown only after you connect (your country only if you choose to show it). Your exact budget is never shown.
  • Interests are limited per day, so that each one means something.

9. How homes are ordered

We order homes by how well they fit the search you gave us (place, price, rooms, type and your switches), how good and complete the listing is, how new it is, and a little variety so that new homes and sellers are seen. Paying never changes this order. If we ever show a “Featured” home, it is labelled, it fits your search, and there is at most one in every fifteen cards.

10. Fees

SEVI is free during the pilot. If we add paid features later, you will see the price and what you get before you pay, and nothing you already have becomes paid without your agreement.

11. Reports and moderation

  • We review reports. We may remove content and restrict, suspend or close accounts that break these terms or the law. When we do, we tell you what we did and why, and you can ask us to look again. People who repeatedly break the rules lose their accounts.

16. Law and disputes

Greek law applies, without taking away the protection that the law of the country where you live gives you as a consumer. Disputes go to the competent Greek courts, unless the law lets you use the courts where you live. Consumers can also turn to the Hellenic Consumer Ombudsman (Συνήγορος του Καταναλωτή).
