1. Who is responsible

Sevi (operator details to be confirmed) is responsible for your personal data on SEVI (the “controller”). You can reach us through Help & feedback in your Profile, or by email to privacy@sevi.homes. If we are not established in the EU, our representative in the EU will be named here.

2. What we collect

  • Your account: your first and last name, your email address or phone number, how you sign in, a photo of you, your language, and when you accepted these texts.
  • From Apple, Google, Facebook or Microsoft, if you sign in with them: your name, your email address and an account ID, as you allow. We never take your photo from them.
  • If you buy: your search (places, budget, rooms, type of home and your switches, such as Golden Visa or Flexible matching), the homes you like or pass, your interests, and your buyer card (what you are looking for, your timeline, a short introduction, your languages and, if you choose, your country).
  • If you sell: your listing (the address and exact location, which stay private, photos, details, price, energy certificate number and description) and your verifications (your phone number, the result of an identity check, and a document that shows you own the property).
  • Connections: who you are connected with, and, during the pilot, the introduction emails we send. When chat arrives, the messages and photos you send.
  • Use of the app: which homes were shown to you and what you did, app events, crash reports, your device type and app version, a notification token if you allow notifications, and IP addresses in security logs.
  • What we never collect: your age (only that you are 18 or older), your nationality or your income. We use your device's location only if, as a seller, you tap “I'm at the property now”, and only for the pin you confirm.

3. Why we use it, and on what legal basis

  • To provide SEVI to you: your account, homes that fit the search you gave us, interests, Connections and introductions. Basis: our contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To improve SEVI and keep it safe: learning which homes to show first from your likes and passes, checking the quality of listings, preventing fraud and spam, keeping SEVI secure and measuring how it is used (our own analytics). Basis: our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)). You can object to this; when it is available, the “Use only my filters” switch stops SEVI learning from your swipes.
  • Only with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time: marketing messages, showing your listing's photos on our social media and analytics tools from other companies. During the pilot we do none of these.
  • Identity checks with a selfie, if we use them: only with your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)).
  • When the law requires it, for example to answer a lawful request from an authority (Art. 6(1)(c)).

4. Who can see your data

  • Other users, before you are connected: a seller sees a buyer's first name, budget band, areas of interest and buyer card, never the buyer's photo, last name, email address or phone number. Buyers see a seller's first name, last initial and photo next to the listing, and an approximate location, never the exact address.
  • After you are connected: you both see each other's full name and photo, and the exact address of the home. During the pilot, SEVI's team introduces you by email, so you also share your email addresses with each other.
  • Our service providers, who process data only on our instructions and under data processing agreements: Supabase (our database, sign-in, file storage and server functions, on Amazon Web Services in Frankfurt, Germany), Amazon Web Services (sending emails, from Frankfurt), Twilio (sending SMS codes) and Expo (delivering notifications). Later, possibly: crash and analytics tools hosted in the EU, an identity-check provider and map providers (Apple Maps or Google Maps, for a seller's pin).
  • Apple, Google, Facebook and Microsoft, when you sign in with them, and the app stores: they act under their own privacy policies.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.
  • We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

5. Where your data is kept

Your data is stored in the EU, in Frankfurt, Germany. Some of our providers are based in the United States. Where they may access data from outside the European Economic Area, we rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework if the provider is certified, or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long we keep it

During the pilot, the scheduled deletions below start with the public launch. Until then we delete by hand, and at the latest when the pilot ends: original photo uploads, ownership documents, passes, the record of homes shown to you and app events. A replaced profile photo, and your data when you ask us to delete your account, go as described below.

  • Your account and your search: while you have an account. After 24 months without use, we warn you and then delete it.
  • A profile photo you replace: deleted at once.
  • Listing photos: the original uploads 30 days after they are processed; the photos shown in the app while the listing exists, and up to 90 days after it is sold, expires or is deleted.
  • Ownership documents: 90 days after the listing ends. Identity checks: only the result and the provider's reference.
  • Passes: 12 months. Which homes were shown to you: 90 days. App events: 13 months. After that, only totals that no longer identify you.
  • When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your data without undue delay, apart from what the law requires us to keep and the following. App events stay, no longer linked to your account, for up to 13 months from when they happened. The record of a Connection stays on the other person's side, no longer linked to your account. Reports you made or that were made about you, with what was reported, and our team's log of its actions on accounts and listings stay for as long as needed for moderation and safety. Security logs, which include IP addresses, are kept only as long as needed for security. Backups, where kept, roll over within 7 days.

7. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data (including the preferences we have inferred), to correct it, to delete it, to restrict its use, to receive it in a portable format, and to object to uses based on our legitimate interests. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Ask through Help & feedback, or by email to privacy@sevi.homes; we answer within one month. You can also complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr) or to the authority where you live.

8. How we protect it

Your data travels encrypted. Photos and documents are kept in private storage. The database itself decides what each person may see, so other people's private details never reach your phone. Access by our team is limited and logged. On your phone, your sign-in is kept in the system's secure storage (the Keychain or the Keystore).

9. Automated ordering

SEVI orders homes automatically, by how well they fit your search, how good the listing is and how new it is (see the Terms of Use, §9). This never makes a decision about you with legal or similarly significant effects. If we offer enhanced photos, they are made with an AI image tool and always labelled.

10. Adults only

SEVI is for people aged 18 or older.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change applies.

Questions about this policy? Write to privacy@sevi.homes.
