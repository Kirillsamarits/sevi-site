1. Who is responsible

Sevi LLC, a company in formation that is being registered in Athens, Greece, is responsible for your personal data on SEVI (the “controller”). Our postal address: Spartis 11, 165 61 Glyfada, Athens, Greece. We will add the company's registration and VAT numbers here once it is registered. You can reach us through Help & feedback in your Profile, or by email to privacy@sevi.homes.

2. What we collect

  • Your account: your first and last name, your email address or phone number, how you sign in, a photo of you, your language, and when you accepted these texts.
  • From Apple, Google, Facebook or Microsoft, if you sign in with them: your name, your email address and an account ID, as you allow. We never take your photo from them.
  • If you buy: your search (places, budget, rooms, type of home and your switches, such as Golden Visa or Flexible matching), the homes you like or pass, your interests, and your buyer card (what you are looking for, your timeline, a short introduction, your languages and, if you choose, your country).
  • If you sell: your listing (the address and exact location, which stay private, photos, details, price, energy certificate number and description) and your verifications (your phone number, a document that shows you own the property and, once we add identity checks after the pilot, the result of one). If you use Tidy up on a photo, also the areas you brushed and the edited photo.
  • If you scan your home with an iPhone or iPad that has LiDAR: the floor plan you save (the rooms, walls, doors and windows in metres, their approximate areas, and the scanning device's model) and a 3D model of the rooms you scanned.
  • Connections: who you are connected with, and the emails our team sends you about a connection: an introduction where our team introduces you, or a reminder where messaging is switched on and neither of you has written. Where messaging is switched on: the messages you send, an email address, phone number or video call link you choose to share, and when you last read a conversation.
  • Use of the app: which homes were shown to you and what you did, app events (such as which screens you opened and whether a step worked), error reports from the app, your device type and app version, and IP addresses in security logs. When we add them: crash reports from other tools.
  • If you turn on notifications: your phone's notification token, your phone's time zone (so we never send at night), the app version, and which kinds of notification you switched off.
  • If you join the waitlist on sevi.homes: your email address, whether you are selling or looking, the area you choose, the page's language, and when you agreed. You don't need an account. The form sets no cookies. When it is sent, our hosting provider's security logs record your IP address.
  • What we never collect: your age (only that you are 18 or older), your nationality or your income. We use your device's location only if, as a seller, you tap “I'm at the property now”, and only for the pin you confirm.

3. Why we use it, and on what legal basis

  • To provide SEVI to you: your account, homes that fit the search you gave us, interests, Connections and introductions. Basis: our contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To let you message the people you connect with, where messaging is switched on: we store and deliver your messages, show the other person when you have read theirs, and let them know that a new message is waiting. Basis: our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Safety reminders in messages: if what you are writing looks like it contains a phone number, an email address or a link, the app shows a short reminder. This is worked out on your phone. Nothing about it is sent to us or stored.
  • To handle reports: when someone reports a message, our team reads that message and the report, to decide what to do. Basis: our legitimate interest in keeping SEVI safe, and our legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(f) and (c)).
  • To tidy up a photo when you ask us to: when you use Tidy up, we send only that photo and the areas you brushed to our photo editing provider, which removes what you brushed, and we keep the edited photo with your listing, next to the original. You decide for each photo, and you can always keep the original. Basis: our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To correct light and colour when you ask us to: we correct light, contrast and colour ourselves, on the servers in Frankfurt that store your listing, without sending the photo to any other provider. Basis: our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To make a floor plan when you scan your home: the scan runs on your phone, and nothing leaves it until you tap Save. Then we store the floor plan and the 3D model with your listing, on our servers in Frankfurt, and show buyers the floor plan. Areas are approximate, never a certified measurement, and the size you entered never changes unless you change it. Basis: our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To check listings follow our rules: automated checks, a price comparison and photo fingerprints, described under “How we check listings” below. Basis: our legitimate interest in keeping listings accurate and lawful, and our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(f) and (b)).
  • To let our team act on listings from email: we email our review desk a listing's public details and photo links (valid for up to 7 days), never your address, name or contact details: a photo our check flags as showing people or private details, and a description with contact details or links, are left out of the email. Basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To help you write your description (only when you ask): when you tap “Suggest with AI”, we send your listing's public details, listed in the next point, to Google's Gemini AI, which drafts a description for you to check and edit. Basis: our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • What a suggested description uses: your listing's type; area and region; living and plot size; bedrooms and bathrooms; floor and number of floors; year built and renovated; condition; heating; parking; features; whether it is occupied; and energy class. Never your address, exact location, price, name, contact details, documents or photos.
  • To improve SEVI and keep it safe: learning which homes to show first from your likes and passes, checking the quality of listings, preventing fraud and spam, keeping SEVI secure and measuring how it is used (our own analytics). Basis: our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)). You can object to this; when it is available, the “Use only my filters” switch stops SEVI learning from your swipes.
  • To send you notifications, only if you turn them on: news about your listing, interest in your home, Connections, new messages and new homes for your search. Basis: our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)). You can turn each kind off in Profile → Notifications, or all of them in your phone's settings.
  • To tell you when the beta opens for you (the waitlist): with your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). We use the address for this only, and the area you choose to plan where the beta opens next. You can withdraw at any time by writing to support@sevi.homes; that does not affect what we did before.
  • Only with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time: marketing messages, showing your listing's photos on our social media and analytics tools from other companies. During the pilot we do none of these.
  • Identity checks with a selfie, if we use them: only with your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)).
  • When the law requires it, for example to answer a lawful request from an authority (Art. 6(1)(c)).

How we check listings

  • The automated check. When you submit a listing, Google's Gemini AI looks at its photos and public details for things our rules don't allow, such as people or house numbers in photos, misleading edits or contact details in the text. It also checks that the photos suit a home listing and match the details you gave.
  • What it reads. The public details are: its type; region, area and municipality; price and whether you are open to offers; living and plot size; bedrooms and bathrooms; floor and number of floors; year built and renovated; condition; heating; parking; features; whether it is occupied; energy class; Golden Visa eligibility; headline (listings created before November 2026) and description, with its language.
  • What it never receives: your address, exact location, name, email, phone number or documents, unless you wrote them into the headline or description yourself, which our rules don't allow, or they are visible in a photo, which is what the check looks for.
  • The price comparison. On our own servers, we compare your price per m² with reference prices for the area (such as official objective values or published market price indices or, until we have verified those, our own estimates) and with a national range.
  • At the price step. When you enter your price, the app shows how it compares. If it is far from what similar homes are listed at, you can submit it only after changing it or adding a short note that explains why, and our team reads that note before the listing goes live.
  • Our estimate. The automated check also gives our team its own estimate of the usual price per m² for a home like yours, from the public details above. We may show you a range based on it, always called our estimate; it never decides anything by itself.
  • Price advice. About two days after your listing goes live, we compare its price per m² with similar SEVI listings in the area and with the verified reference prices (and only if our other checks agree). If it is above or below the range we show you, we tell you once, in the app and by email, as advice; your listing stays live.
  • Photo fingerprints. To spot copied listings, we compute a fingerprint of each photo you upload (a short code worked out from the picture, which stays with SEVI and never leaves the EU) and compare it with the photos in other sellers' listings.
  • Who decides. If every check passes, your listing can go live automatically; otherwise a person on our team reviews it. A person makes every refusal or request for changes.

4. Who can see your data

  • Other users, before you are connected: a seller sees a buyer's first name, budget band, areas of interest and buyer card, never the buyer's photo, last name, languages, where they are based, email address or phone number. Buyers see a seller's first name, last initial and photo next to the listing, and an approximate location, never the exact address.
  • After you are connected: you both see each other's full name and photo, and the seller also sees the languages the buyer speaks and where they are based, if the buyer has shared it. The exact address of the home is not shown in the app.
  • Messages, where messaging is switched on: only you and the person you are connected with can read your conversation. Your email address, phone number or a video call link reach them only if you choose to share one, and a shared detail can't be taken back. Our team reads a message only when it is reported, or when the law requires it.
  • Introductions by email: if you connected while messaging was not switched on, SEVI's team introduces the two of you by email, so you share your email addresses with each other. If you connected while it was switched on, we do not: your email address reaches the other person only if you share it yourself.
  • Reminders by email: where messaging is switched on and neither of you has written a few days after you connected, our team may email each of you separately to remind you. We use the email address of your account for this. It is not shown or sent to the other person.
  • Our service providers, who process data only on our instructions and under data processing agreements: Supabase (our database, sign-in, file storage and server functions, on Amazon Web Services in Frankfurt, Germany), Amazon Web Services (sending emails, from Frankfurt), Twilio (sending SMS codes) and Expo (delivering app updates and notifications). Later, possibly: crash and analytics tools hosted in the EU, and an identity-check provider.
  • Notifications, only if you turn them on: Expo's push service passes each notification to Apple Push Notification service (iPhone) or Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android), which deliver it to your phone. They receive your phone's notification token, its title and text (at most a home's area; never an address, a price, a message's text, or anyone's name or contact details) and internal codes for the screen it opens. They use these only to deliver it, as our processors.
  • Maps: Apple Maps (iPhone) or Google Maps (Android) show the map when you place your home's pin. They receive the map area shown and your IP address, never the address you typed.
  • Floor plans: buyers see the floor plan of a live listing (its rooms, walls, doors, windows and approximate areas), never its address, location or 3D model; a floor plan is drawn in the scan's own metres, not on a map. Our team sees the floor plan, the 3D model and the scanning device's model, to check them. No other company receives them: they are stored by Supabase, as above.
  • Our photo editing provider, Google Cloud (Vertex AI, Gemini image models), only for a photo you choose to tidy up: it receives that photo and the areas you brushed, and nothing else about you or your listing.
  • Our listing check and writing provider, Google Cloud (Vertex AI, Gemini models): for the listing check it receives your listing's photos and public details (and returns its findings and a price estimate), and for a suggested description only the public details listed in §3.
  • For both, Google Cloud works only on our instructions, under a data processing agreement, and may not use what it receives to train its models. It processes it in the EU and keeps it for no more than 24 hours, unless it must keep it longer to investigate abuse of its service. If we ever use a model or provider that is only offered outside the EU, we will update this policy and say so before it is used.
  • Apple, Google, Facebook and Microsoft, when you sign in with them, and the app stores: they act under their own privacy policies.
  • Waitlist entries: only our team sees them. They are stored by Supabase and, when we write to you, the email is sent through Amazon Web Services, as above. No other company receives them.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.
  • We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

5. Where your data is kept

Your data is stored in the EU, in Frankfurt, Germany. Some of our providers are based in the United States. Where they may access data from outside the European Economic Area, we rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework if the provider is certified, or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Photos you tidy up are processed by our photo editing provider in the EU today. If a photo is ever processed outside the EU (see §4), for example in the United States, the transfer will be protected: Google LLC is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, and Google's data processing terms also include the Standard Contractual Clauses.

Listing checks and suggested descriptions (see §3) are processed in the EU today. If they are ever processed outside the EU (see §4), for example in the United States, the transfer will be protected: Google LLC is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, and Google's data processing terms also include the Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long we keep it

During the pilot, the scheduled deletions below start with the public launch. Until then we delete by hand, and at the latest when the pilot ends: original photo uploads, ownership documents, passes, the record of homes shown to you, app events, the record of notifications sent and rejected notification tokens. A replaced profile photo, and your data when you ask us to delete your account, go as described below.

Documents you hand to our team directly during the pilot, such as a signed consent or an ownership document, are kept in our team's private storage until the pilot ends, then deleted.

  • Your account and your search: while you have an account. After 24 months without use, we warn you and then delete it.
  • A profile photo you replace: deleted at once.
  • Listing photos: the original uploads 30 days after they are processed; the photos shown in the app, edited and original, while the listing exists, and up to 90 days after it is sold, expires or is deleted.
  • Edited photos: if you tidy up a photo, the areas you brushed are kept with the edited photo for as long, so our team can check the edit, and our photo editing provider keeps what it received for no more than 24 hours, unless it must keep it longer to investigate abuse of its service. If you undo an edit, the edited photo and the areas you brushed are deleted.
  • Floor plans and 3D models: while the listing exists, and deleted with it or with your account. Scanning again replaces them, and you can remove them at any time.
  • Listing check results, including the price comparison, the price estimate, the check about two days after going live, your note on the price and the photo fingerprints: with the listing, and up to 90 days after it ends (a fingerprint goes with its photo). Our team's review decisions, with the reasons we gave you, are kept with them.
  • Links in review emails: expire after at most 7 days; the one-click actions after 72 hours.
  • Suggested descriptions: a record of each request (which listing, which language, when) for 90 days, and the suggested text itself for 24 hours. A description you keep is part of your listing.
  • Ownership documents: 90 days after the listing ends. Identity checks: there are none during the pilot. Before we add them, we will tell you in the app what we ask for and how long we keep it, and update this policy.
  • Notifications: your phone's notification token until you sign out on that phone (if it is offline then, until another account signs in on it), until you delete your account, or until the phone stops accepting our notifications (we then stop using it at once).
  • Notification records: the record of each notification sent (which kind, when, whether it arrived): 90 days. Apple, Google and Expo keep a notification only until it is delivered or expires.
  • Conversations: your messages are kept while the connection is open and for 24 months after it closes (the home is sold, the connection ends or one of you blocks the other). Then the conversation is deleted, automatically, during the pilot too. If you delete a message for everyone, a private copy is kept for 90 days for moderation, then deleted.
  • Your messages after you delete your account: they stay in the other person's conversation, without your name, until that conversation is deleted. Contact details and video call links you shared there are removed at once. If one of them was reported, the report keeps it (see "What else stays").
  • Passes: 12 months. Which homes were shown to you: 90 days. App events: 13 months. After that, only totals that no longer identify you.
  • Waitlist entries: until you withdraw, and at most 12 months after you joined or last joined again; then deleted. The security logs are kept as said below. A waitlist entry is separate from an account: deleting an account does not remove it, so write to support@sevi.homes to leave the list.
  • When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your data without undue delay, apart from what the law requires us to keep and the following. App events stay, no longer linked to your account, for up to 13 months from when they happened. The record of a Connection stays on the other person's side, no longer linked to your account.
  • What else stays: reports you made or that were made about you, with what was reported (the reported message, or the contact detail or link that was shared and reported), and our team's log of its actions on accounts and listings stay for as long as needed for moderation and safety. Security logs, which include IP addresses, are kept only as long as needed for security. Backups, where kept, roll over within 7 days.

7. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data (including the preferences we have inferred), to correct it, to delete it, to restrict its use, to receive it in a portable format, and to object to uses based on our legitimate interests. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

Ask through Help & feedback, or by email to privacy@sevi.homes; we answer within one month. You can also complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr) or to the authority where you live.

8. How we protect it

Your data travels encrypted. Photos and documents are kept in private storage. The database itself decides what each person may see, so other people's private details never reach your phone. Access by our team is limited and logged. On your phone, your sign-in is kept in the system's secure storage (the Keychain or the Keystore).

9. Automated ordering

SEVI orders homes automatically, by how well they fit your search, how good the listing is and how new it is (see the Terms of Use, §9). This never makes a decision about you with legal or similarly significant effects.

Automated checks can approve a listing that passes all of them; they never refuse or change a listing by themselves, and a person makes every refusal or request for changes. You can ask a person to look again at any decision at support@sevi.homes.

When a seller edits a photo, with Tidy up (an AI image tool that removes only what the seller brushed) or with our own light and colour correction, the photo is labelled “Edited” and buyers can always see the original.

10. Adults only

SEVI is for people aged 18 or older.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change applies.

Questions about this policy? Write to privacy@sevi.homes.
