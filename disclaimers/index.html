1. SEVI connects people

SEVI connects buyers and sellers of homes in Greece. We are not an estate agent, broker, lawyer, notary, engineer, valuer, tax adviser or financial adviser, and we are not a party to any sale. We don't take part in negotiations, hold money or deposits, or promise that a sale will happen.

2. Listings come from sellers

Sellers write their listings and take their photos. We check every listing before it goes live, and we may ask for documents, but we can't check everything and we don't guarantee that a listing is accurate, complete, up to date, lawful or still available. Automatic approval and the price comparison reduce risk; they don't confirm that a listing is accurate or that the seller owns the home.

Sizes, prices, the energy class, permits, the legal status of the property and anything else in a listing must be checked by you and your advisers. Edited photos are labelled “Edited”, and you can always see the original.

3. Locations are approximate

Until you are connected, a home's location on SEVI is approximate on purpose, to protect the seller. Areas and distances are a guide only.

4. Golden Visa

Information about the Greek Golden Visa (the residence permit for property investors) is general information, never advice. Eligibility depends on the property, its location, its use, the price, how it is paid for and the law at the time you apply, and the law has changed before.

A Golden Visa label on a listing is based on what the seller tells us and on the thresholds we know of; it does not confirm that you will be eligible. Independent legal advice is required.

5. Legal, tax and financial information

Anything SEVI shows about laws, taxes, costs or financing is general and may be out of date. It is not legal, tax or financial advice. The taxes, fees and costs of buying in Greece depend on your situation.

6. Do your own checks

Before you commit to anything:

  • see the home, in person or by video;
  • use a lawyer to check the title, any debts or charges on the property and its record with the Hellenic Cadastre (Κτηματολόγιο);
  • use an engineer to check permits, any unauthorised building work and the condition of the building;
  • use other licensed professionals where you need them, such as a notary, an accountant or a valuer;
  • check who you are dealing with, and never send money to anyone you haven't verified. SEVI will never ask you for a deposit.

7. Other users

We verify how people sign in and some of their details, and a badge shows what has been verified. We can't vouch for anyone's intentions. Be careful, and report anything that seems wrong.

Messages are written by users, not by SEVI, and we don't read them unless one is reported. Share a contact detail only when you are ready, and never send money or a deposit because someone asks for it in a message.

8. Prices and figures

Prices are the sellers' total asking prices in euros. Any conversion into another currency is a guide only. Counts, estimates and descriptions of how well a home fits your search are a guide, not a promise. A floor plan a seller scanned with their phone, and the areas on it, are approximate references, not a certified measurement or survey: check sizes when you visit.

9. The pilot

During the pilot, SEVI may change, stop working for a while or lose test data.

These disclaimers don't take away any rights you have by law (see the Terms of Use, §13).
